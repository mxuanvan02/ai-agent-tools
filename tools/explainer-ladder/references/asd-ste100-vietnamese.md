# ASD-STE100 kieu tieng Viet cho tai lieu day hoc

ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English) la ngon ngu co kiem soat viet cho tai
lieu bao duong hang khong. Y tuong loi: nguoi doc khong phai vat lon voi cau chu
de con dung suc hieu noi dung. Ap dung tinh than do cho tieng Viet nhu sau.

## Quy tac cau

1. Moi cau duoi 20 tu. Moi cau dung mot y.
2. Dung the chu dong. Viet "hay tinh dao ham", khong viet "dao ham can duoc tinh".
3. Mot khai niem - mot thuat ngu. Da chon "trong so" thi khong doi sang "tham so"
   o giua bai.
4. Khong dung tu viet tat tu che. Neu bat buoc dung, giai nghia ngay lan xuat hien
   dau tien va dung nhat quan ve sau.
5. Thu tu thong tin: dieu kien truoc, hanh dong sau. "Neu mang mat ket noi, hay
   khoi dong lai ung dung" - khong dao nguoc.

## Quy tac doan va cau truc

1. Moi doan toi da 6 cau, mot chu de duy nhat.
2. Quy trinh trinh bay bang danh sach danh so; moi buoc mot hanh dong gioi han.
3. Noi ket qua hoac hanh dong ke tiep truoc, giai thich chi tiet sau.
4. Uu tien tu thuan Viet trong van ban day hoc: thang danh gia (khong dung
   "rubric" tro), cau lenh (khong dung "prompt"), bang dieu khien (khong dung
   "dashboard"). Giu thuat ngu goc tieng Anh trong ngoac khi can doi chieu tai
   lieu quoc te, lan dau tien ma thuat ngu xuat hien.

## Muc "80% ASD-STE100"

Dung khi chu de chuyen sau va giong van qua cung lam mat tinh tu nhien:

- Giu: cau ngan, the chu dong, thuat ngu nhat quan, cau truc ket-qua-truoc.
- Noi long: cho phep cau ghep vua phai (toi da 2 menh de), cho phep doan dai
  hon khi mach lap luan doi hoi.
- Khong noi long: khong quay lai van phong dich thuat dai dong, khong tron nhieu
  tu dong nghia.

## Vi du doi sang kieu viet kiem soat

Truoc (dich thuat, dai dong):

> Viec thuc hien danh gia su hieu biet cua nguoi hoc doi voi cac khai niem
> co ban cua mon hoc thong qua bai kiem tra co the duoc tien hanh boi giang vien.

Sau (ASD-STE100 tieng Viet):

> Giang vien danh gia nguoi hoc bang bai kiem tra. Bai kiem tra do muc hieu
> cac khai niem co ban cua mon hoc.

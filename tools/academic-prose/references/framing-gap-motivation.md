# Framing the Gap and Motivation Without a Strawman

The motivation and gap statement carry a hidden claim about the reader. Getting
the literal words right is not enough — read what the sentence IMPLIES about the
practitioners in the field, because that implication is what a referee reacts to.

Two blocking patterns, both author-confirmed on the HUL law-assessment paper:

## 1. Strawman gap

Do not manufacture the research gap by attributing to practitioners an ignorance
of something obvious in their own field. Measured, author-rejected: the draft
implied law teachers cannot tell "correct per the textbook" from "correct per
the law invoked", and that a superseded provision could not still govern a past
situation — both elementary to anyone trained in law.

Why it fails: it makes the contribution trivial (it solves a non-problem) AND
insults the readership. A referee strikes it immediately.

Repair: recast the gap as *tacit expert knowledge not yet made explicit /
verifiable / reproducible / transferable at scale*, never as *a thing the reader
does not know*. This shifts the paper from "teaching the obvious" to
"systematizing + validating + making auditable" — the correct register for a
methods paper. Concretely, the deficit became: judgment is not written down, so
it cannot be checked, reproduced across reviewers, reused when an item is
re-deployed, or offered as programme-outcome evidence.

## 2. Defensive negation leaks the accusation

Writing "practitioners are of course fully capable of X, so the difficulty is
not that they lack X…" is self-incriminating: the very act of denying it proves
the author once framed them as lacking X. Nobody doubted it, so raising it is
both redundant and still insulting. Author's exact correction: *"đừng kiểu
'thừa sức phân biệt nguồn…' chả có ai nghi ngờ về điều đấy, khi viết phải
hiểu hàm ý thể hiện ra là gì."*

Repair: state the problem DIRECTLY and objectively, with the tool/system/process
as the grammatical subject, never the person. Example that passed: "thẩm định
câu hỏi là công việc dựa trên phán đoán chuyên môn, và phán đoán ấy chủ yếu
diễn ra trong đầu người thẩm định, ít khi được ghi thành tiêu chí tường minh…"
Never mention whether people "know" or "don't know" anything.

## Rule of thumb

Make the DEFICIT a property of process, artifacts, or scale (no explicit
criteria, no question dossier, no audit trail, no role split between legal and
measurement expertise), not a property of the people. Watch these carriers of a
hidden slight, all found and fixed on this paper:

| Leaked implication | Objective recast |
| --- | --- |
| "nhiều giảng viên chưa được đào tạo về đo lường" | "đo lường giáo dục là một chuyên môn riêng" |
| "thực tế giảng viên chưa được đào tạo hệ thống" | "không thuộc đúng lĩnh vực của câu hỏi, dù nắm vững chuyên môn của mình" |
| "giảng viên không chỉ thông báo đáp án mà còn…" (ám chỉ hiện làm qua loa) | "hồ sơ cho phép chỉ ra bước lập luận bị bỏ qua…" (chủ ngữ là công cụ) |

## Propagation

Changing this framing is a base-layer change: propagate in ONE pass through every
dependent slot before rebuilding — abstract (both languages), the motivation
paragraph, each related-work closing sentence, the gap statement, the
contribution section, and the conclusion. A half-changed frame reads as
self-contradiction. Rebuild and re-run the word-count / audit gates only after
the whole pass, since each edit shifts the body count against the venue cap.

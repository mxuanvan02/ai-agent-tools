# Item review form

Undergraduate law course: six-criterion framework.

A. BASIS FOR THE ANSWER KEY; mandatory, considered before every criterion.
Several bases combined? State the relation between them.

B. TWO-DIMENSION SPECIFICATION: cognitive level and professional operation.

C. SIX CRITERIA x FOUR RECORDING LEVELS; mark one cell per criterion.
Criterion 5 is a pre-examination sweep: a failing mark here signals work to revise,
not evidence already confirmed.

C1. DISTRACTOR ERROR MAP: every distractor must carry one misconception;
reuse it as teaching material when reviewing the item.

D. VERDICT: check in order, stop at the first matching row.
The no-offset principle applies to every row.

Supporting documents: complete or incomplete (recorded in block D). If not, assess criterion 6 in block C.
